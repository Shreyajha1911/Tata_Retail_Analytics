Revenue Drivers and Growth Risks at Tata Online Retail
A Power BI analytics project analysing customer value segmentation and revenue concentration for a UK-based online retailer. Built as part of the Tata Virtual Experience Programme on Forage.

## The business question

## "Where is revenue coming from, and what are the risks hiding inside that growth?"

The dataset covers 12 months of online retail transactions. The goal was to identify which customers, products, and markets drive the most revenue — and where the business is most exposed.

## Key findings

Total revenue: £9.75M across 19,313 orders from 4,373 customers
High-value customers generate £8.7M — approximately 90% of total revenue despite being only 37% of the customer base
Medium-value customers contribute £0.9M and low-value customers just £0.2M
A small loss of high-value customers could cause a disproportionate revenue drop — the single biggest business risk identified
Revenue peaked sharply in mid-2011 before declining — a seasonal or churn-related pattern worth investigating
Order volume is heavily concentrated in Europe, with minimal penetration in other regions
Top product by sales volume: Regency Cakestand 3 Tier (362M units)


## Dashboard pages
** Page 1 — Executive Overview **

Key metrics at a glance: total revenue, orders, customers, and average order value. Includes revenue trend over time, order volume by country map, top 5 products by revenue, and customer segment breakdown.

** Page 2 — Customer Segment Deep Dive **
Show Image
Breaks down revenue by High, Medium, and Low value customer segments. The core insight: high-value customers are not just the biggest segment — they are almost the entire business. Losing even a fraction of them would be catastrophic.
